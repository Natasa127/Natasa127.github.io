I am a Masters student in Computer Science at the University of Toronto, where I am honoured to be working under the supervision of Sheila McIlraith. I completed a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and in Mathematics at the University of Ottawa, where I had the great pleasure of working under the supervision of Tom Cesari and Roberto Colomboni.

My research interests lie in theoretical AI, particularly sequential decision-making under uncertainty, with an emphasis on fairness and social welfare. My previous research has focused on online learning in brokerage settings with limited feedback. Currently, I am interested in AI alignment, continual learning, and multi-agent systems.
